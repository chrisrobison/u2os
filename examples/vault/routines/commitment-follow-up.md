Look at what I've promised people, and help me stay on top of it.

1. Go through my open commitments in the vault. For each, note who it's
   to, what I promised, and when it's due.
2. Flag anything overdue first, then anything due in the next two days.
3. For each flagged commitment, draft a short, friendly follow-up or
   status update to the person it's owed to, in my own voice. Draft only;
   do not send anything.
4. Send me one notification: the drafts waiting for my review, and a short
   list of everything else still open but not yet due.

If nothing is due or overdue, tell me that in one line and stop there.
