---
name: Meeting prep
when:
  event: calendar.event_approaching
---
A meeting is coming up. Prepare me for it.

1. Look up the calendar event: what it's about, when it starts, and who is
   attending.
2. For each attendee I have a vault record of, check who they are to me,
   any open commitments between us, and the last few notes or interactions
   I have about them.
3. Put together a short briefing: what the meeting is about, who's in it
   and how I know them, anything I owe them or they owe me, and one or two
   things worth raising.
4. Send me the briefing as a notification. If I have no useful history
   with anyone attending, say so plainly rather than padding it out.

Do not modify the event, respond to it, or contact any attendee yourself.
